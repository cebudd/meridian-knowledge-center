About this role

As a Medical Science Liaison, you are Meridian's scientific face in the field. You build peer relationships with key opinion leaders, you respond to unsolicited scientific inquiries, you gather insights that inform our medical strategy, and you advance Meridian's reputation as a science-driven partner to the medical community.

You do not promote. You do not sell. You report to Medical Affairs leadership, and your incentive structure is decoupled from prescribing volume by design. This separation is what gives our scientific exchange credibility — protect it.

Travel & expense

MSL territories require regular travel. Your role assumes roughly 50% time in the field, depending on territory geography and KOL distribution.

Travel booking

All Meridian travel is booked through Concur. Domestic flights default to economy class for flights under 6 hours. Hotels are booked at the Meridian-preferred rate; the property list is in Concur.

Per diem & meals

Per diem rates follow GSA guidelines for the destination city. Meals with HCPs are governed by separate limits and documentation requirements — see the HCP Interactions Policy.

Expense submission

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurring them. Late submissions delay reimbursement and create audit findings.

Field equipment: Your iPad, MiFi hotspot, and badge printer will be shipped to your home address before Day 1. If anything is missing on your first day, message @Lumen in Teams.

Career development

Field Medical at Meridian is a recognized career path, not a stepping stone. The typical progression:

  • MSL — your role on hire
  • Senior MSL — typically 3+ years, with demonstrated KOL leadership and cross-territory influence
  • Lead MSL / Field Trainer — mentoring new hires and contributing to MSL curriculum
  • Regional Medical Director — managing an MSL team (Daniel's path)
  • VP, Field Medical — TA-level leadership

Lateral moves into Global Medical Affairs, Medical Information, or Clinical Development are encouraged and common.

Frequently asked questions

What if I don't have all my access on Day 1?

Lumen tracks every access request in real time. The vast majority of new hires are fully provisioned by Day 1. If something is missing, message @Lumen in Teams and they'll resolve it before you can open a separate IT ticket.

Can I co-promote with my Sales counterpart?

No. MSLs do not promote. You can attend a joint visit at an HCP's request, but your scientific exchange must be clearly separated from the Sales rep's promotional content. See Scientific Exchange vs. Promotional.

What's my call quota?

Field Medical does not operate on a call quota. We measure engagement quality, KOL coverage, insight generation, and TA expertise. Your Regional Medical Director will work with you on goals that fit your territory.

How do I handle an off-label question?

Pause the conversation, capture the question verbatim, and respond per Meridian's unsolicited request SOP (typically a follow-up within 5 business days with approved scientific information). See the field guide.

Who do I talk to about a compliance concern?

Several options: your manager, your HR Business Partner, the Medical Affairs Compliance helpdesk, or the anonymous Meridian Voice line. See the Code of Conduct.

What if I want to publish or present at a congress?

External publication and presentation activity goes through the Medical Affairs Publications team. Submit your concept via the Publication Planning portal. Authorship and disclosure rules apply.

How does my performance review work?

Annual cycle with quarterly check-ins. Your performance signals are pulled automatically from Veeva (engagement metrics), the LMS (training completion), and feedback from your Regional Medical Director and KOL relationships. Lumen will surface a draft self-assessment for you to refine.
