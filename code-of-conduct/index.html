Meridian Code of Conduct

Our commitment to ethics, integrity, and patient safety.
Effective January 1, 2026 · Version 3.1

Every Meridian employee makes hundreds of decisions every day. Each one is an opportunity to live out our values — or to fall short of them. This Code is the practical guide we use to make sure our decisions reflect who we are: a science-driven, patient-centered company that competes on the strength of our innovation and the integrity of our conduct.

— Letter from the Chief Executive Officer

Our values

Five values guide everything we do at Meridian Biopharma:

Patient at the heart

Every decision starts with the patient. When the choice is unclear, the patient's interest is the tiebreaker.

Bold

We take measured risks in pursuit of science that changes lives. We do not take shortcuts on safety, ethics, or quality.

One Meridian

We work as one team across functions, geographies, and brands. Internal competition does not serve patients.

Inclusive

We respect every colleague, partner, and customer regardless of background, role, or perspective. Diverse teams build better medicines.

Act for progress

We choose action over analysis paralysis. We learn from setbacks and we share what we learn.

Our responsibilities

To patients and healthcare professionals

We market our medicines accurately and within their approved indications. We engage with healthcare professionals in compliant, transparent ways. We respond to medical inquiries promptly and with balanced scientific information. We never offer or imply inducements to prescribe.

To Meridian and each other

We protect Meridian's confidential information and intellectual property. We safeguard the personal data of our colleagues, patients, study subjects, and partners. We use Meridian resources responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes.

To our shareholders and the public

We maintain accurate financial records. We comply with all applicable laws, including the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, the UK Bribery Act, anti-trust laws, and securities regulations. We do not trade on material non-public information.

To our communities and the planet

We comply with environmental and safety regulations. We pursue Meridian's sustainability commitments in our operations, supply chain, and product lifecycle. We engage as responsible corporate citizens in the communities where we operate.

Compliance essentials

Anti-corruption & anti-bribery

We do not offer, give, solicit, or accept anything of value to influence a business decision. This includes interactions with healthcare professionals, government officials, payers, and commercial partners. Even the appearance of impropriety is a problem.

Interactions with healthcare professionals

All HCP interactions must comply with the PhRMA Code, applicable national codes, and Meridian's internal SOPs. Meals, hospitality, and educational support are governed by specific limits and documentation requirements. See the HCP Interactions Policy for thresholds.

Scientific exchange

Medical Affairs colleagues — including Medical Science Liaisons — engage in scientific exchange with HCPs in response to unsolicited requests. See Scientific Exchange vs. Promotional for the working framework.

Data privacy

We comply with GDPR, HIPAA, and applicable national privacy regulations. Personal data is collected for specified purposes, retained only as long as necessary, and protected through appropriate technical and organizational measures.

Conflicts of interest

You must disclose any personal financial interest, outside activity, or relationship that could compromise — or appear to compromise — your judgment on behalf of Meridian. Annual disclosure is mandatory; supplemental disclosure is required whenever circumstances change.

Speaking up

If you see something that doesn't feel right, you have an obligation to raise it. You have several channels:

  • Speak with your manager or HR Business Partner
  • Contact Meridian Compliance directly: compliance@meridianbiopharma.com
  • Use the Meridian Voice line — anonymous, available 24/7 in your local language
  • For ethics matters affecting patient safety, page the Medical Safety Hotline

Non-retaliation. Meridian prohibits retaliation against any employee who raises a concern in good faith, regardless of whether the concern is ultimately substantiated. Retaliation is itself a Code violation.

Living the Code

The Code cannot anticipate every situation. When you face a decision that's not explicitly covered, ask yourself:

  1. Is it legal?
  2. Is it consistent with Meridian's policies and values?
  3. Would I be comfortable if my decision appeared on the front page of a newspaper?
  4. Would I be comfortable explaining it to a patient, my family, or my manager?

If you answer no to any of these — or if you're unsure — stop and ask for guidance before you act.
