Setting up your MSL territory in Veeva: KOL views, account plans, and call planning

📄 Owner: Medical Affairs Operations 🕐 8 min read 📅 Last updated: May 14, 2026 👁️ 894 views

Overview

This guide walks new Medical Science Liaisons through the first-week setup of their Veeva Medical CRM territory. By the end you will have logged in, validated your pre-loaded KOL stakeholder list, configured the views you'll use day-to-day, and successfully logged your first practice call.

Your territory has already been provisioned by the People Operations onboarding workflow. Everything below assumes your access went live on Day 1.

If you don't see Veeva on your launchpad on Day 1: message @Lumen in Teams. Don't open a separate IT ticket — Lumen already has the context and will fast-track resolution.

Prerequisites

  • Meridian M365 account active (you're already signed in if you can read this)
  • Veeva Medical CRM provisioned (check the access list in your welcome email)
  • Mobile device enrolled in Intune (required for iPad Veeva access in the field)
  • FDA Promotional Compliance Training scheduled (Day 2 — gates your independent HCP interactions)

Step-by-step

1Accessing Veeva Medical CRM for the first time

Open veeva.meridianbiopharma.com and sign in with your Meridian SSO credentials. The first login will take 30–60 seconds while your territory is hydrated. You'll land on the Medical Affairs home page — not the Commercial sales page. If you see the commercial view, your role hasn't propagated yet; wait 10 minutes and refresh.

On mobile, install the Veeva CRM app from the Meridian MAM portal (not the public App Store). Authenticate with the same SSO credentials.

2Reviewing your pre-loaded KOL stakeholder list

Your KOL list was pre-loaded by Medical Affairs Operations based on your territory assignment. To see it:

  1. Click My Accounts in the left nav.
  2. Filter by Account Type = HCP — KOL.
  3. You should see between 25 and 60 KOLs depending on territory density.

For each KOL, you'll find their affiliation, specialty, publication history, prior engagement notes from your predecessor (if any), and consent status for unsolicited scientific exchange.

Spot-check before your first field interaction: if a KOL is missing or you spot a duplicate, do not edit the master record directly. Submit a correction request via the Stakeholder List Correction form in Veeva so changes are audited and propagate to Snowflake.

3Configuring your call planning view

Veeva ships with a generic call planning view. Replace it with the Field Medical view your team uses:

  1. In Call Planning, click the gear icon and select Switch View.
  2. Choose Medical Affairs — Quarterly Engagement Plan (default).
  3. Set your default cycle to the current quarter.

This view weights KOLs by tier, surfaces upcoming congress activity, and integrates with your Outlook calendar so call scheduling becomes a single-click action.

4Logging your first call

After every HCP interaction — in-person, virtual, or phone — you'll log a Medical Call Record. The minimum required fields are:

  • HCP: auto-filled from your call plan or searched manually
  • Date/time: defaults to now
  • Channel: in-person, video, phone, email
  • Topic discussed: select from the Medical Affairs topic library (do not free-text)
  • Materials shared: link to approved scientific exchange materials only
  • Follow-up commitments: what you owe them, with a due date

Calls must be logged within 24 hours per Meridian's Medical Affairs SOP. The Veeva mobile app supports voice-to-text capture — most field-experienced MSLs log on the drive back.

5Linking your account plan

An account plan is the strategic narrative for each KOL or institution — what you're trying to learn, what scientific exchange is on the table, and what their evolving informational needs look like. Your manager will work through your top-tier accounts with you in your Week-2 1:1.

For now, just confirm each Tier-1 KOL has a draft account plan attached. If any are missing, flag them in your Day-5 readiness check with Daniel.

Before your first field call

  • Veeva login confirmed on desktop AND mobile
  • KOL stakeholder list reviewed end-to-end
  • Call planning view switched to Medical Affairs default
  • Practice call logged (use the sandbox HCP record)
  • FDA Promotional Compliance Training complete
  • Account plans drafted for all Tier-1 KOLs

You're ready when: you can find a KOL, open their record, schedule a call from the call plan, and log the interaction afterward without consulting this guide.
